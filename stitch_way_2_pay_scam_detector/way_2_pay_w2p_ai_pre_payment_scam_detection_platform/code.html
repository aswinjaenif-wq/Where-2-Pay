support_agent

Fake Customer-Care Scams

What it looks like: Fraudulent support numbers posted on search engines claiming to resolve banking or parcel queries.

Warning sign: Representative requests you to download AnyDesk or approve an incoming reverse payment request.

Safe Action: Use in-app verified help centers only.
qr_code_scanner

QR Code Inversion / Swap

What it looks like: Malicious stickers pasted over genuine merchant QR codes at counters or on parking meters.

Warning sign: Display name on payment confirmation screen differs from the physical store name.

Safe Action: Verify merchant display name before PIN.
shopping_bag

Spoofed Shopping Gateways

What it looks like: Social media ads offering 80% discounts leading to near-pixel-perfect fake storefronts.

Warning sign: Domain age under 14 days, lacking legitimate merchant registration records or postal addresses.

Safe Action: Scan checkout domain inside W2P.
gavel

Impersonation & Authority Scams

What it looks like: Threatening callers posing as police, immigration, or tax auditors claiming immediate arrest warrants.

Warning sign: Demands immediate settlement through crypto, instant gift cards, or personal wire transfers.

Safe Action: Law enforcement never demands instant transfers.
currency_exchange

Instant Refund / Overpayment

What it looks like: A buyer claims to have accidentally sent extra money and asks you to refund the difference.

Warning sign: The incoming proof is a fabricated screenshot; funds were never actually credited to your ledger.

Safe Action: Check your official bank statement independently.
local_shipping

Urgency & Delivery Phishing

What it looks like: "Your package is detained at our hub due to unpaid $1.50 customs tax. Pay now or item is returned."

Warning sign: Link directs to a dynamic proxy page asking for card details and full CVV.

Safe Action: Never pay release fees from unsolicited SMS.
